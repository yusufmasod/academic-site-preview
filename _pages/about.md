I am a **Senior Lecturer in Printing Technology** at the Faculty of Art & Design, Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Cawangan Selangor, Kampus Puncak Alam.

My work focuses on printing technology, print-production systems, digital prepress workflows, and print quality control. I also study how artificial intelligence, technological change, and workforce development are shaping printing and related manufacturing industries in Malaysia.

## Research interests

- Artificial intelligence adoption in printing and manufacturing
- Digital prepress workflows, PDF standards, and production systems
- Print quality evaluation, proofing, and process control
- Printing-industry competitiveness and technological transition
- Printing education, workforce development, and industry collaboration

## Selected work

### Malaysia Printing Industry Monitor

A data-based resource tracking sales, employment, and wages in Malaysia’s printing industry from 2019 to 2025.

### PrintQC Lab: interactive print-quality learning

An open learning toolkit supporting Solid Ink Density (SID), Tone Value Increase (TVI), CIELAB colour validation, dot uniformity, and laboratory scenario simulation.
