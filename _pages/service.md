My professional service connects printing education, industry practice, workflow standards, and academic development.

## Professional engagement

### Ghent Workgroup (GWG)

I am professionally affiliated with the Ghent Workgroup, an international organisation supporting standards and best practices for graphic-arts workflows. Universiti Teknologi MARA is listed as an Educational Member.

[Visit the Ghent Workgroup](https://www.gwg.org/)

## Industry engagement

My engagement with the Malaysian printing industry supports:

- Collaboration between industry and printing education
- Awareness of technological and workforce changes
- Student exposure to contemporary production environments
- Internship placement and host-organisation evaluation
- Knowledge exchange relating to digital workflows and print quality

## Student and internship support

I support students in comparing potential internship organisations based on learning value, supervision, technical exposure, organisational suitability, and programme expectations.

## Academic contribution

My academic contribution includes research dissemination, teaching-resource development, student supervision, and participation in activities supporting printing technology and related creative industries.
