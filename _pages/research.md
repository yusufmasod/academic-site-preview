My research examines how printing technology, digital workflows, and industry structures are changing through technological transition, automation, and evolving production practices.

## Artificial intelligence in printing and manufacturing

This research examines how artificial intelligence is being introduced into printing and related manufacturing environments. It considers industrial applications, operational benefits, organisational readiness, integration challenges, and financial constraints.

**Selected publications**

- Masod, M. Y., & Zakaria, S. F. (2024). Artificial Intelligence in the Printing Industry: A Systematic Review of Industrial Applications, Challenges and Benefits. [View publication](https://doi.org/10.47772/IJRISS.2024.8090141)
- Masod, M. Y., & Zakaria, S. F. (2024). Artificial Intelligence Adoption in the Manufacturing Sector: Challenges and Strategic Framework. [View publication](https://doi.org/10.47772/IJRISS.2024.81000013)

## Digital prepress workflows and print quality

This work focuses on reliability and control in digital production workflows, particularly PDF standards, proofing, verification, and print-quality management.

**Selected publications**

- Masod, M. Y., Zainuddin, A. A., & Azim, I. N. A. (2023). An Approach to Quality-Managed Proofing. [View publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCAIE57739.2023.10165452)
- Masod, M. Y., & Zainuddin, A. A. (2022). Procedure Enhancement for PDF Output Workflow Conformity. [View publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCAIE54458.2022.9794464)
- Masod, M. Y., Hassan Nazri, N. S., & Nordin, N. (2021). PDF Output Workflow Conformance Test to the PDF/X-4 Standard. [View publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSPC53359.2021.9689185)

## Printing-industry development and education

This research considers how technological change affects industry competitiveness, professional skills, workforce development, internships, and industry–education collaboration in Malaysia.

**Selected publications**

- Masod, M. Y. (2026). Internship as Organizational Strategies: Talent, Tacit Knowledge and Identity Work in Malaysia’s Printing Industry. [View publication](https://doi.org/10.55197/qjssh.v6i6.891)
- Masod, M. Y., & Zakaria, S. F. (2023). The Health and Outlook of the Printing Industry Post-Covid-19. [View publication](https://doi.org/10.14622/JPMTR-2225)
